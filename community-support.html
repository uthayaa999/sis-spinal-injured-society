Our Community Values

நமது சமூகத்தின் மதிப்புகள்

  • Respect and dignity for every member.
  • Equal opportunities and accessibility.
  • Mutual support without discrimination.
  • Privacy and responsible information sharing.
  • Independence and informed decision-making.
  • Safe and inclusive community participation.
