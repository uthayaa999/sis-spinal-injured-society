Healthcare & Rehabilitation Services

🏥

Physiotherapy & Rehabilitation

Physiotherapy helps people work towards individual movement, comfort and functional goals after spinal cord injury.

🤝

Occupational Therapy

Occupational therapy supports participation in everyday activities using individualized strategies and adaptations.

📋

Personalized Care Planning

Prepare a coordinated care plan with qualified professionals, based on individual goals and needs.

📦

Medical Kit & Consumables

Plan supply needs with your clinical team. SIS intends to support future needs assessment; no kit request or allocation is active here.

💚

Sexual & Reproductive Health

SCI may affect sexual function and fertility. Individual experiences differ. Confidential professional guidance can support informed choices, consent and relationships.

♿

Emergency Information

In a medical emergency, seek immediate medical care. Do not wait for a website response.

National Emergency: 112
